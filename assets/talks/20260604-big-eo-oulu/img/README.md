## Conceptual graphics

The two "data wall" charts on the asymmetry slide and every architectural diagram (COG, Zarr, STAC, Pangeo, ARD, data cubes, end-to-end stack) are drawn **inline as SVG** in `index.Rmd` — no external files needed.

## Notes

- Each image is credited on its slide via the `.credit` line under the photo, or a `.cc-bottom-left` line for full-bleed hero slides.
- The `common-crawl-logo.svg` is the official logo from commoncrawl.org. If Common Crawl's branding changes, re-download from their site.
- If you swap to higher-resolution photos, prefer Wikimedia thumbnails at the standard widths (320, 640, 800, 1024, 1280, 1920, 2560).
